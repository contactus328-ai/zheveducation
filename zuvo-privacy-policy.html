ZUVO – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 13 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zhev Education (“Zhev Education”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the ZUVO mobile application (“ZUVO” or the “App”), a platform for discovering and engaging with college and university events.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use ZUVO, we may collect information such as your name, email address, mobile number, college or institution, academic information you choose to provide, event registrations and attendance information, device and technical information, IP address, and information about how you use the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create, authenticate and manage accounts.
  • Provide event discovery, registration and related services.
  • Send registration confirmations, event reminders, event updates and important service communications.
  • Connect users with relevant colleges, institutions and event organisers.
  • Maintain security, prevent misuse and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Improve the App and user experience.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Information Shared With Event Organisers and Institutions

When you register for or participate in an event, information required to administer that event may be shared with the relevant organiser, college or institution. Depending on the event, this may include your name, contact information, college or institution and registration details.

4. Notifications and Communications

We may send account, registration, event and service-related notifications. You can control optional notification permissions through your device settings.

5. Location Information

If ZUVO uses location-based features such as nearby-event discovery, the App will request the relevant permission and explain the purpose of access. Location information will only be used for features that require it and according to the permissions you grant.

6. Third-Party Services

ZUVO may use third-party providers for authentication, hosting, analytics, notifications, security and other technical services. Such providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction. No electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide ZUVO services, maintain legitimate records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and comply with applicable legal requirements.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your ZUVO account and associated personal information, subject to information that we are required or permitted to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention or other legitimate purposes. Contact us using the details below and provide the email address associated with your account.

10. Children’s Privacy

ZUVO is designed primarily for students and participants in college and university events. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, update or request deletion of certain personal information, and other rights relating to consent or processing. Requests can be made using the contact details below.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes may be communicated through the App or other appropriate means. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
