ZUVO Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 13 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zhev Education collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the ZUVO mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use ZUVO, we may collect your name, email address, mobile number, college or institution, academic information you choose to provide, event registrations and attendance information, device and technical information, IP address and information about how you use the App.

2. How We Use Information

  • Create, authenticate and manage accounts.
  • Provide event discovery, registration and related services.
  • Send registration confirmations, reminders, updates and important service communications.
  • Connect users with relevant colleges, institutions and event organisers.
  • Maintain security, prevent misuse and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Improve the App and user experience.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Sharing With Organisers and Institutions

When you register for or participate in an event, information required to administer that event may be shared with the relevant organiser, college or institution. This may include your name, contact information, college or institution and registration details.

4. Notifications

We may send account, registration, event and service-related notifications. Optional notification permissions can be controlled through device settings.

5. Location

If ZUVO uses location-based features such as nearby-event discovery, the App will request the relevant permission and explain the purpose of access. Location information will be used for features that require it and according to the permissions granted.

6. Third-Party Services

ZUVO may use third-party providers for authentication, hosting, analytics, notifications, security and other technical services. Such providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.

7. Security and Retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain legitimate records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and comply with law.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your ZUVO account and associated personal information, subject to information we are required or permitted to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention or other legitimate purposes. Contact Zhev Education using the contact details on the Contact page.

9. Children’s Privacy

ZUVO is designed primarily for students and participants in college and university events. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

10. Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, update or request deletion of certain personal information and other rights relating to consent or processing.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
